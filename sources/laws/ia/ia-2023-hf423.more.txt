1 340B PROGRAM - COVERED ENTITIES AND CONTRACT PHARMACIES, §510D.6 510D.6 Applicability.
This chapter shall apply to covered entities, contract pharmacies, group health plans, health carriers that offer group or individual health insurance coverage, third-party administrators, and pharmacy benefits managers, but shall not apply to their operations under a contract with the state Medicaid agency or a Medicaid managed care organization, regardless of whether the covered entity or contract pharmacy is eligible to retain the 340B discounts generated by the covered entities and contract pharmacies. 2023 Acts, ch 43, §7 Wed Nov 20 23:33:02 2024 Iowa Code 2025, Section 510D.6 (1, 0)
