Section 1: Executive Summary
This report is submitted pursuant to Idaho Code § 41-351, which requires covered entities
participating in the federal 340B Drug Pricing Program to annually report financial and program
data to the Department of Health and Welfare, Office of the State Controller, and Office of the
Attorney General. The Office of the State Controller aggregates the data and prepares a report
for the Legislative Council. The data presented herein covers the period of January 1 through
December 31, 2025 (Calendar Year 2025).

The 340B Drug Pricing Program, established under Section 340B of the Public Health Service
Act (42 U.S.C. § 256b), requires pharmaceutical manufacturers to provide outpatient drugs to
eligible health care organizations and covered entities at reduced prices. The program is
designed to stretch scarce federal resources and enable eligible organizations to provide more
comprehensive services.

Total aggregate payments received ($812.9 million) exceed aggregate acquisition costs ($399.1
million), yielding a gross program margin of approximately $413.7 million. This margin represents
the financial benefit generated through 340B pricing, which covered entities are required to
document, and report how they utilize gross savings from participating in the 340B program
under Idaho Code § 41-351.

For Calendar Year 2025, Idaho covered entities collectively reported nearly 1.92 million
prescription drugs obtained under the 340B program and dispensed to patients, generating over
$812.9 million in aggregate payments received, while directing $430.6 million in 340B savings
toward charity care, community benefit programs, and services for indigent patients.

Section 2: Statutory Authority & Background

Idaho Code § 41-351
Idaho Code § 41-351 establishes the reporting framework for covered entities participating in the
340B Drug Pricing Program operating within the State of Idaho. The statute requires each
covered entity to submit an annual report to the Department of Health and Welfare, Office of the
State Controller, and Office of the Attorney General, containing the following mandatory data
elements:

   •     Aggregate Acquisition Cost: The aggregate acquisition cost paid by the covered entity
         for 340B drugs, broken down by payer type.
   •     Aggregate Payments Received: Total payments received from all payers for 340B
         drug claims, broken down by payer type.

    •   Aggregate Payments to Contract Pharmacies: Total payments made to contract
        pharmacies for dispensing 340B drugs, broken down by payer type.
    •   Number of Claims by Payer Type: Total number of claims dispensed under the 340B
        program, categorized by payer type (commercial, Medicare, Medicaid, self-pay,
        uninsured, and others)
    •   Use of 340B Savings: How 340B savings were applied, including charity care,
        uncompensated care offsets, patient financial assistance, prescription assistance,
        community health programs, and other qualifying uses.

340B Drug Pricing Program
The 340B Drug Pricing Program was created by Congress in 1992 under Section 602 of the
Veterans Health Care Act of 1992. It requires pharmaceutical manufacturers to sell outpatient
drugs to eligible health care organizations at discounted prices in exchange for Medicaid
coverage of their products. The program is administered by the Health Resources and Services
Administration (HRSA) within the U.S. Department of Health and Human Services.

Eligible covered entities include federally qualified health centers (FQHCs), Ryan White HIV/AIDS
Program grantees, disproportionate share hospitals (DSH), critical access hospitals (CAH), rural
referral centers (RRC), sole community hospitals, children's hospitals, free-standing cancer
hospitals, and certain other safety-net providers and federal grantee organizations. Idaho's
participating entities represent a cross-section of these categories, serving rural, underserved,
and indigent populations across the state.

Section 4: CY2025 340B Program Data

Idaho Code § 41-351 requires covered entities to report the aggregate acquisition cost incurred
for 340B drugs, categorized by payer type. This represents the actual cost paid by covered
entities to acquire 340B-priced medications. For CY2025, the total aggregate acquisition cost
across all Idaho covered entities was $399,146,308.13. The table below summarizes
acquisition costs by payer type:

Number of Claims by Payer Type
Pursuant to Idaho Code § 41-351, covered entities are required to report the total number of
340B drug claims dispensed, broken down by payer type. For CY2025, Idaho covered entities
collectively reported 1,916,995 total claims across all payer categories. Commercial insurance
was the dominant payer category, accounting for 45.4% of all claims, followed by Medicare at
31.1%. The following table summarizes the number of claims by payer type:

Section 5: Conclusion
The CY2025 data submitted pursuant to Idaho Code § 41-351 demonstrates that Idaho's 340B
covered entities collectively process nearly 1.92 million claims, generating $812.9 million in
aggregate payments received for 340B drugs from payers and patients, with an acquisition cost
of $399.1 million for the drugs, and directs $430.6 million in 340B program savings toward
community benefit programs.
